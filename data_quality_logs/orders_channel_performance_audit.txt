BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AUDIT: SALES CHANNEL UNIT ECONOMICS
============================================================

QUERY EXECUTED:
SELECT
  channel,
  COUNT(order_id) AS num_orders,
  ROUND(SUM(net_revenue), 2) AS total_net_revenue,
  ROUND(SUM(gross_revenue), 2) AS total_gross_revenue,
  ROUND(SUM(net_revenue) / NULLIF(COUNT(order_id), 0), 2) AS net_aov,
  ROUND(SUM(gross_revenue) / NULLIF(COUNT(order_id), 0), 2) AS gross_aov,
  ROUND(AVG(profit), 2) AS avg_profit,
  ROUND((COUNTIF(returned = True) / NULLIF(COUNT(returned), 0)) * 100, 2) AS return_rate
FROM `ecommerce_profitability_dw.raw_orders`
GROUP BY channel;
